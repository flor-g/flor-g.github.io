# To Give the Illusion of Free Will

Flora Gu

---

A friend who works in the gaming industry once told me that the most important quality that a well-designed game must possess is that a player when playing the game has the illusion of free will—that even if the game follows a set of pre-determined routes, it nevertheless engages the player in a way such that they feel as if they have full control over their choice in this game. I think the same principle applies to any well-designed application, and especially a well-designed device that helps a person with disability gain autonomy. As such, in addition to optimizing how accurately the device decodes user intent, it is also important to optimize the UX so that the user feels that they own the device as if the device is but another (physical or mental) limb of their own as opposed to an alien extension which they do not have control over.
